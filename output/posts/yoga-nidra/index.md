### What is Yoga Nidra?

Yoga Nidra, stemming from the traditions of Tantra Yoga, is a systematic form of guided relaxation that has a profound effect on your body, mind, and emotions. It's often practiced lying down with a teacher guiding the session. The practice navigates the borderline state between sleep and wakefulness, aiming to induce a deep state of relaxation while maintaining consciousness. This section will delve into the origins, principles, and stages of Yoga Nidra, setting the foundation for a deeper understanding of its practice.
