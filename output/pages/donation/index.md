### Who We Are

Jhuggi Jhopdi Shiksha Sewa Samiti is a non-profit organization putting heart and soul in providing modern education along with meditation to the underprivileged kids residing in Jhuggi Jhopdis (hutments) and slums in India. Our organization has been religiously working to reduce the educational disparity and ensure a brighter future for our kids.

![](images/3-2-1-2.png)

### Who Gets Help

At Jhuggi Jhopdi Shiksha Sewa Samiti, we believe that education goes beyond any financial or social background, and every child has a born right to it. This is an initiative to make education accessible to everyone and ensure that no one is deprived of a chance to gain knowledge and grow.

![](images/1-2-2.png)

### What We Do

We want to empower children in our care with not just knowledge but also provide them with the wisdom of meditation.

![](images/img-1-2.png)

### How are we impacting Education?

We go beyond old-school textbook teaching and learning. Here at Jhuggi Jhopdi Shiksha Sewa Samiti, we are all about nurturing young minds through worldly knowledge, but also guiding them on a journey of inner growth and enlightenment through meditation.

### How are we impacting lives?

Our core mission centers on safeguarding the youth from the often unforgiving challenges of the world, guiding and paving their way to a brighter future. Along with proper education, we also care for their hearts and minds, so their dreams stay safe and their minds strong!

### How are we impacting Health?

At Jhuggi Jhopdi Shiksha Sewa Samiti, we believe that good health is the foundation for a better future. We prioritize our students and their families’ physical well-being and therefore organize regular free health check-ups.

## FAQs

Explore common queries about the Science Divine School and our mission to provide quality education and empowerment to underprivileged children.

1\. What is the mission of the Science Divine school?

The mission of the Science Divine school is to provide quality education and holistic development opportunities to underprivileged children. We are dedicated to breaking the cycle of poverty and ensuring that every child, regardless of their background, has access to a bright future through education. Our aim is to empower these young minds with knowledge, values, and skills that will help them succeed in life, contribute to their communities, and realize their full potential. Through education, we envision a world where every child has the opportunity to thrive and build a better tomorrow.

2\. How long has the Science Divine school been operational?

The Science Divine school has been operational for over two decades, with a rich history of serving the educational needs of underprivileged children. Established more than 20 years ago, our commitment to providing quality education and making a positive impact on the lives of students has remained steadfast throughout this journey. Over the years, we have touched the lives of thousands of students, helping them build a brighter future through education and holistic development programs.

3\. What is the school's policy on child protection and safeguarding?

The Science Divine school is unwavering in its commitment to child protection and safety. With robust policies and dedicated staff, we ensure a secure environment for all students, fostering their growth with confidence and care.

4\. What is the NGO school's impact on the community it serves?

In the community we serve, the NGO school’s impact is profound. Over two decades, we’ve empowered thousands of students, breaking barriers with quality education and fostering a brighter future.

5\. What is the background and experience of the faculty members?

Our dedicated faculty members bring diverse backgrounds and extensive teaching experience to our school. They’re passionate about providing quality education and nurturing students’ growth.
