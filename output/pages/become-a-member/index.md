## FAQs

1\. What is the Science Divine Foundation's Membership Program?

The Science Divine Foundation’s Membership Program is an exclusive initiative that allows individuals to become a part of our spiritual community, offering various benefits and opportunities for personal growth and development.

2\. How can I join the Membership Program?

To join the Membership Program, simply visit our website and follow the easy registration process. Once registered, you’ll have access to the program’s benefits and privileges.

3\. Is there a membership fee?

No, our Membership Program is absolutely free. We believe in making spiritual knowledge and community support accessible to everyone.

4\. What are the benefits of being a member?

As a member, you’ll have the chance to volunteer in our organization, attend monthly meet-ups with Sakshi Shree Ji, and gain free access to select courses and kriyas.

5\. Can anyone join the Membership Program?

Yes, the Membership Program is open to all individuals who are interested in spirituality, self-improvement, and contributing to a positive community.
