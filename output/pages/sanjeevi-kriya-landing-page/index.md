## What is Sanjeevni Kriya?

Immortality Meditation holds the key to a transformative journey towards love and divinity. It is not merely an act of meditation or doing something, but rather a practice that guides you towards stillness and doing nothing. However, achieving this state is no easy task, as the mind tends to wander even in moments of apparent relaxation. The path from confusion and unclarity of mind to a state of no-mind passes through meditation, ultimately leading to the opening of existence itself. This practice not only brings inner peace, soundness of body and mind, but also helps in realising one’s true self.

Many people report remarkable shifts during the retreat—renewed perspective, fresh insight, a clearer mind. These few days also make the rest of your year more alive, productive, and full of energy. When you emerge, you feel centered and refreshed, ready to take on life with greater focus and joy.

The retreat is an ideal way to reconnect with your practices and the experience of SKY Breath Meditation and take your energy to another level.
